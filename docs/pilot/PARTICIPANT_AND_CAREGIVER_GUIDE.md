# Participant and caregiver guide\n\n## A simple explanation for the participant\n\n“This is Mori, a computer program that can talk with us about photos and memories you have chosen. Mori can make mistakes. I will stay here with you. You do not have to remember anything, and you can pause or stop whenever you want. Would you like to try it today?”\n\nAsk this at every session. A verbal or nonverbal refusal ends the session even when a representative has provided consent.\n\n## What caregivers should expect\n\n- Sessions last no more than 20 minutes during the proposed pilot.\n- A trained supervisor remains close enough to see and hear the participant.\n- Mori may speak about approved memories and may offer a quiet conversation.\n- Mori must not diagnose, test memory, provide medical advice, handle emergencies, or replace human contact.\n- The participant can pause, change the memory, turn voice off, or finish at any time.\n- Caregivers can mark a memory helpful, neutral, sensitive, or unsuitable and can withdraw sharing.\n\n## Stop and provide human support when\n\n- The participant says no, asks to leave, pulls away, repeatedly resists, or appears frightened.\n- Distress continues after one gentle redirection.\n- Mori uses the wrong person's information, exposes private content, or behaves outside the approved script.\n- A medical, safeguarding, missing-person, abuse, or self-harm concern arises.\n- The supervisor is uncertain whether continuing is appropriate.\n\nFor urgent concerns, follow the site's human emergency or safeguarding process. Mori does not contact emergency services.\n\n## After a session\n\nAsk whether the participant feels comfortable and would want to use Mori again. Review any summary before sharing it. Record corrections, interventions, technical problems, distress, refusal, and unexpected behavior without adding unnecessary private detail.\n\n## Privacy choices\n\nPhoto display, microphone processing, transcript storage, caregiver sharing, and optional research use are separate choices. Participation is voluntary.
