# Consent, assent and media permissions

Consent must describe Mori's AI nature, purpose, supervision, foreseeable emotional and privacy risks, alternatives, voluntary participation, data recipients, vendors, retention, deletion, recording choices, withdrawal and contact routes.

Record separately:

- Patient consent when the person has decision-making capacity
- Legally authorized representative consent and basis of authority when applicable
- Participant assent at every session
- Photo/video display permission
- Microphone/audio processing permission
- Transcript storage permission
- Caregiver sharing permission
- Optional de-identified research-use permission

A present refusal, resistance, fear, repeated request to leave, or other clear dissent ends the session regardless of representative consent. Research-use permission must not be bundled with access to ordinary care. The pilot site determines capacity and legally authorized representative requirements under applicable law and, if research, the IRB-approved process.
