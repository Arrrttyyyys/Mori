# Supervisor training and session checklist

## Before each session

- Verify consent/representative authorization and ask for the participant's assent today.
- Confirm the participant is comfortable and not acutely unwell or unusually confused.
- Review sensitive/blocked people, topics and media.
- Test pause, stop, microphone and network recovery.
- Keep local emergency, safeguarding and pilot safety-lead contacts available.

## During the session

Remain present and able to hear and see the participant. Never leave Mori to manage distress or an emergency. Do not coach memory performance or correct harmless inaccuracies. Protect dignity; avoid infantilizing language.

Escalation ladder:

1. Slow down; use one short, concrete idea.
2. Validate emotion without confirming an unsafe belief.
3. Remove or change the stimulus.
4. Pause and offer to stop.
5. End the session and provide human support.
6. Follow the site's emergency or safeguarding procedure when required.

## After the session

- Confirm the participant is settled.
- Review and correct the summary before sharing.
- Rate the stimulus helpful, neutral, or avoid.
- Record interventions, distress, technical failures and unexpected behavior.
- Escalate incidents before another session when required by the protocol.
