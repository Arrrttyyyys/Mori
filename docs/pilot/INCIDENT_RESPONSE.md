# Incident and escalation response

## Categories

- Safety: self-harm, abuse, medical concern, missing-person risk, severe distress
- Privacy/security: unauthorized access or disclosure, wrong-patient content, lost device, credential compromise
- Product: unsafe response, failed stop control, repeated outage, corrupt or missing record
- Protocol: session without valid consent/assent or supervision

## Immediate response

Protect the participant first; stop Mori; summon the human supervisor; use local emergency or safeguarding procedures. Do not rely on Mori as emergency dispatch. Preserve relevant logs without copying unnecessary patient data.

## Handling

Critical incidents immediately pause affected use and enrollment. Notify the safety lead and privacy lead. Document time, people involved, observed facts, system version, actions and outcome. Determine required patient, site, regulator, insurer, vendor or law-enforcement notification with qualified counsel. Complete root-cause analysis, corrective action, regression testing and written authorization before resuming.
