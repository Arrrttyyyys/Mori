# Mori supervised-pilot pack

Mori's first real-world use is a **supervised usability and safety pilot**, not autonomous care, diagnosis, psychotherapy, or evidence that dementia is treated. No participant may be enrolled until every required launch gate is signed off.

## Proposed pilot

- 5–10 adults with mild-to-moderate dementia
- 4–6 weeks; 2–3 sessions per week; 10–20 minutes per session
- A trained caregiver or staff supervisor physically present for every session
- A named dementia-care clinical advisor and named safety lead
- No medication, diagnosis, emergency triage, efficacy, or disease-progression claims

## Documents

- `PILOT_PROTOCOL.md` — objectives, eligibility, workflow, measurements and stopping rules
- `SUPERVISOR_GUIDE.md` — training and in-session escalation ladder
- `CONSENT_AND_ASSENT.md` — consent, representative authority, assent and media permissions
- `INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md` — safety, privacy and technical incident process
- `DATA_MANAGEMENT_PLAN.md` — access, retention, deletion, vendors and auditability
- `LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` — accountable go/no-go gate

These are working operational templates, not legal or medical advice. The pilot site, qualified counsel, privacy lead and clinical advisor must adapt and approve them for the jurisdiction and population.
