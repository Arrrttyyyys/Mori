# Supervised-pilot go/no-go checklist

The accountable pilot lead signs only after evidence is linked for every required item.

## Product and safety

- [ ] Durable production storage; no in-memory patient record path
- [ ] Authentication, role grants and row-level security penetration-tested
- [ ] Pause, resume, remove-stimulus and stop controls pass rehearsal
- [ ] Network/model failure produces a calm, non-repeating fallback
- [ ] Safety scenario suite and manual red-team review pass
- [ ] Caregiver review/correction/deletion workflow verified
- [ ] Model, prompt and scoring versions frozen and recorded
- [ ] Backup restoration and incident drill completed

## Clinical and participant protection

- [ ] Intended use and claims approved
- [ ] Clinical advisor approves protocol, eligibility, exclusions and stopping rules
- [ ] Consent, representative authority and assent process approved
- [ ] Site supervisor training completed and documented
- [ ] Emergency and adult-safeguarding routes confirmed
- [ ] IRB or institutional determination recorded if data may support generalizable research

## Privacy and operations

- [ ] HIPAA/FTC/state-law analysis and required agreements complete
- [ ] Vendor and data-flow review complete
- [ ] Retention, export, correction and deletion procedures approved
- [ ] Breach/incident notification procedure approved
- [ ] Named safety, site, privacy and technical owners available during pilot
- [ ] Staging rehearsal with fictional participants completed
